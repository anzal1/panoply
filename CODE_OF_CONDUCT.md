# Code of conduct

Panoply is built by the people, for the people. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or chat, agrees to these rules.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and explain context they may lack.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
ban anyone who breaks these rules. Report problems privately to the
maintainers through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports
are handled confidentially.
