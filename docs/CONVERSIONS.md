# Panoply conversions

Generated by `conversions/formats.py`. Do not edit by hand.

**214 formats** in 20 families. Panoply reads 212 and writes 156. 3,891 direct converter steps chain into **9,214 format-to-format conversions**, each routed through at most four steps on your device. Only sensible pairs count: same family, a direct bridge, or a known chain such as Word to JPG through PDF. Steps that need an on-device model (transcription, OCR, read aloud) are only used directly, never chained.

Any format converts to any other format in its family. The tables below list the formats, then the bridges between families.
